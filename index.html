Before you decide

Questions owners usually ask

Is it really freehold?+

Yes. Armani Bizcore is freehold — no lease expiry, and you can pass it on, sell it or rent it out.

Can lorries and forklifts work there?+

Units have high roller shutter access, a minimum of 3 dedicated parking/loading bays at the doorstep, 10kN/m² floor loading and passenger-cum-cargo lifts in every block.

What kind of businesses is it suitable for?+

Warehousing & distribution, e-commerce fulfilment, showrooms & galleries, assembly, automotive parts, cold storage, labs, packaging & printing, solar equipment, oil & gas support, IT hardware, furniture, garments, FMCG, agriculture supplies and import/export.

How much is it?+

Pricing depends on the unit type, level and position (corner and frontage units differ). We'll go through the full price list, current packages and financing options with you at your viewing — no obligation.

I'm still renting with a contract. Is it too early?+

Not at all — the earlier you look, the more units you can choose from. Many owners plan the move to line up with their lease ending.
